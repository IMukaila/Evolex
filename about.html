Implementation Sequence

A Six-Month Operating Foundation

A practical, disciplined sequence for making the business easier to understand, manage, and scale.

  1. Phase 01 Months 01 – 02

    Establish Ground Control

    Begin with a verified livestock count, unique non-reusable animal IDs, documented daily feeding routines, and organized employee, supplier, and asset records.

  2. Phase 02 Months 03 – 04

    Make Work Visible & Auditable

    Build dependable logs for health events, feed conversion, veterinary vaccinations, purchasing, inventory, and equipment maintenance so important operational work is never lost.

  3. Phase 03 Months 05 – 06

    Prepare for Commercial Scale

    Leverage standardized procedures and regular performance metrics to onboard institutional buyers, expand downstream processing, and prepare additional farm clusters.
